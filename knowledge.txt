COMPANY PERFORMANCE REPORT
Fiscal Year 2025

Company: NovaTech Solutions
Industry: Technology & Business Software
Headquarters: Casablanca, Morocco
Reporting Period: January 1, 2025 – December 31, 2025

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

NovaTech Solutions is a mid-sized technology company specializing in business software, cloud services, and data analytics solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises.

During 2025, the company experienced strong growth across its main business segments. Total revenue increased by 18.6% compared with 2024, reaching MAD 84.5 million. The growth was primarily driven by increased demand for cloud-based software and data analytics services.

The company also expanded its customer base and entered two new markets in North Africa.

2. FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

NovaTech Solutions generated total revenue of MAD 84.5 million in 2025, compared with MAD 71.2 million in 2024.

Revenue by business segment:

- Cloud Services: MAD 36.8 million
- Business Software: MAD 27.4 million
- Data Analytics: MAD 15.7 million
- Consulting Services: MAD 4.6 million

Operating expenses increased by 12.3% during the year, mainly due to investments in employees, cloud infrastructure, and research and development.

Operating profit reached MAD 14.2 million, representing an operating margin of 16.8%.

Net profit increased from MAD 8.7 million in 2024 to MAD 10.9 million in 2025.

3. CUSTOMER GROWTH

The company ended 2025 with 1,420 active customers, compared with 1,080 customers at the end of 2024.

Customer growth was particularly strong among medium-sized businesses.

The customer retention rate was 91.4%, slightly higher than the 89.7% recorded in 2024.

The average annual revenue per customer increased from MAD 52,400 to MAD 59,500.

4. EMPLOYEE DEVELOPMENT

NovaTech Solutions employed 386 people at the end of 2025, compared with 312 employees in 2024.

The company hired 74 new employees during the year, primarily in software engineering, data science, sales, and customer support.

Employee turnover decreased from 11.8% in 2024 to 9.6% in 2025.

The company invested approximately MAD 2.1 million in employee training and professional development.

5. TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION

Research and development remained a major priority in 2025.

NovaTech invested MAD 6.4 million in research and development, representing approximately 7.6% of total revenue.

The company launched three major products during the year:

- NovaCloud Analytics: a cloud-based business intelligence platform.
- NovaPredict: a predictive analytics solution for sales forecasting.
- NovaAssist: an AI-powered customer support assistant.

NovaAssist became the fastest-growing product in the company's portfolio, reaching 240 customers within its first six months.

6. MARKET EXPANSION

In 2025, NovaTech expanded its operations into Tunisia and Egypt.

The Tunisian market generated MAD 5.8 million in revenue during its first year of operation.

The Egyptian market generated MAD 3.2 million during the final four months of 2025.

Management expects international markets to represent approximately 20% of total revenue by 2027.

7. KEY CHALLENGES

Despite strong financial performance, the company faced several challenges during 2025.

The main challenges included increasing competition from international software providers, rising cloud infrastructure costs, and difficulties recruiting experienced AI and data engineering professionals.

The company also experienced a temporary increase in customer support requests following the launch of NovaAssist.

Management expects these challenges to continue during 2026 but believes that continued investment in automation and employee development will reduce their impact.

8. OUTLOOK FOR 2026

NovaTech Solutions expects revenue to increase between 15% and 20% in 2026.

The company plans to invest approximately MAD 8 million in research and development and intends to hire around 100 additional employees.

Management expects its AI and data analytics products to be the primary drivers of future growth.

The company is also evaluating expansion into Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal.

10. CONCLUSION

NovaTech Solutions achieved strong financial and operational results in 2025.

Revenue, profitability, customer acquisition, and employee growth all improved compared with the previous year.

The company's investments in cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and data analytics are expected to support continued growth in 2026 and beyond.

Management remains confident in the company's long-term strategy and expects international expansion and AI-powered products to become increasingly important sources of revenue.
